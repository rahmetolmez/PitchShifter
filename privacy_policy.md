# Pitch Shifter Privacy Policy

**Tribow Company** · Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pitch Shifter Android app (package `com.tribow.pitchshifter`, the "App"), published by Tribow Company ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: **the App does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data.** Everything happens on your device.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App does not request Internet access and cannot transmit data off your device.

## 2. Audio files you open

When you choose a song using the system file picker, or open an audio file with Pitch Shifter from another app, the App receives access to that one file only. The audio is decoded, pitch-shifted, and played back entirely on your device. The App does not upload, copy to any server, or analyze your files for any purpose other than playback and export. The App may retain permission to re-open the most recently selected file so it can continue playback.

## 3. Exporting and sharing

When you export a pitch-shifted track, the App writes a temporary file to its private cache and saves the result only to the location you choose in the system save dialog. The temporary file is deleted after export. If you use the Share option, the file is passed to the app you select through Android's standard share sheet; any handling by that app is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 4. Data stored on your device

The App stores a single preference locally on your device: whether you prefer dark or light mode. This setting may be included in your device's standard Android backup if you have backups enabled; that backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

## 5. Permissions

- **Foreground service (media playback)** — lets audio keep playing when the App is in the background or the screen is off.
- **Notifications** — shows the media playback notification with play/pause controls. You can turn this off in system settings at any time.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.

## 6. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Since no data is sent to us, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. You can remove all data stored by the App on your device by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

## 8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

## 9. Security

Because your audio and settings never leave your device through the App, they are protected by your device's own security measures.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be reflected before they take effect in an app update.
